On the water, not above it

The house sits on Boulevard Road with a private jetty and eighteen rooms turned toward Dal Lake. Mornings start with kahwa on the deck. By evening the lake goes quiet and the houseboats light up.

Rooms are cedar and plaster, with wool throws and a desk at the window. Breakfast is cooked to order. Dinner, if you want it, is Kashmiri.

Questions guests actually ask

Does every room face the lake?

The three rooms on this page do. The house also has two quieter courtyard rooms that do not. They are not listed here.

Is breakfast really included?

Yes, on both the flexible and the saver rate. Dinner is separate, including the Kashmiri dinner you can add above.

When does free cancellation end?

48 hours before your check-in date, on the flexible rate. The booking card names the exact day as you change dates. The saver rate is not refundable.

Can I work from the room?

Every room has a desk and fibre Wi-Fi that holds video calls. There is also a meeting corner for up to eight, booked with the host.

How do we get from the airport?

About 45 minutes. Pickup is ₹1,800 for one car and can be added to the total on this page. Taxis are easy if you would rather not book it now.

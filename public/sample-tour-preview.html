Day 1 — Touchdown in Srinagar · gardens and lake hour

Meet your driver at Srinagar Airport and transfer to a centrally located hotel near Dalgate (or a deluxe houseboat if you upgraded). Settle in, freshen up, then visit the terraced Mughal gardens — Nishat, Shalimar, and Chashme Shahi — at an easy pace. As the light softens, enjoy a private one-hour Shikara on Dal Lake. Dinner at the hotel; overnight Srinagar.

Day 2 — Drive to Gulmarg · meadow and Gondola

After breakfast, drive via Tangmarg into Gulmarg (about 1.5–2 hours). Check in on or near the meadow. Ride Gondola Phase I to Kongdori when tickets are available locally; Phase II to Apharwat depends on weather and queue. Spend the afternoon on pine trails or at St. Mary’s Church. Dinner and overnight in Gulmarg so you wake on the meadow.

Day 3 — Gulmarg to Pahalgam via the south road

Checkout after breakfast and continue toward Pahalgam (typically 4–5 hours with photo stops). Pause at the Pampore saffron fields in season and the Awantipora ruins. Check in at a Lidder-side hotel. Evening free for a riverside walk and kahwa. Overnight Pahalgam.

Day 4 — Pahalgam valleys — Betaab and Aru

Use a local union cab (paid locally; outside vehicles are not allowed on this circuit) for Betaab Valley and Aru Valley. Optional add-ons the same day: Baisaran meadow or Chandanwari if time and weather allow. Return to the hotel for dinner. Overnight Pahalgam.

Day 5 — Return to Srinagar · crafts and lake views

Drive back to Srinagar (about 2.5–3 hours) and check into your return hotel. Optional visit to Shankaracharya for the wide lake panorama, then Boulevard / Lal Chowk for dry fruit, papier-mâché, and pashmina shopping with vetted shop suggestions on request. Dinner and overnight Srinagar.

Day 6 — Departure transfer

Breakfast at the hotel. Keep a comfortable buffer before domestic flights (we recommend 3 hours). Private transfer to Srinagar Airport. Tour ends.

Why this itinerary works: you sleep in Gulmarg and Pahalgam instead of rushing both as long day trips from Srinagar. That means an early Gondola window and a full valley day without a six-hour return drive.

Stays in this quote (or same category): 1N Srinagar near Dalgate; 1N Gulmarg meadow hotel; 1N Pahalgam riverside; 1N return Srinagar. Optional deluxe houseboat upgrade on night 1.

Travel style: private Innova / Dzire, MAP meals on hotel nights, and clear notes on what you pay locally (Gondola, union cabs, lunches). Exact hotel names are confirmed in writing before you pay the balance.

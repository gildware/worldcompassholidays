The valley, without the brochure fog

Six days, three places, one guide. You sleep on the boulevard, then a night in Gulmarg, then two in Pahalgam. The road between them is part of the trip, and the long one is written in hours, not in adjectives.

Group dates share a vehicle with up to twelve travelers. A private date is your group only. Either way the hotels below are the hotels you get, and the total on this page includes GST.

The six days

Breakfast is at the hotel. Dinner is at the hotel except the shikara evening, which includes dinner on return. Lunch is not included.

1 Arrive, then the lakeSrinagar · no drive with us · dinner · Dal Lake House +
Afternoon

You land at Sheikh ul-Alam and come to Boulevard Road on your own, or with the airport add-on. The room is ready from 14:00. Bags can wait if you are early.

Evening

A one-hour shikara from the house jetty, before the boulevard gets loud. Kahwa on the deck when you are back, then dinner.

2 Old city and the gardensSrinagar · 1 hour of driving · breakfast and dinner · Dal Lake House +
Morning

Jama Masjid and the old-city lanes on foot, about 3 km at an easy pace, with time to stop. The guide keeps the group together at the gates.

Afternoon

Shalimar Bagh and Nishat Bagh. Entry is included. You are back at the house before the evening rush on the boulevard.

3 Up to the meadowGulmarg · 2 hours in the car · breakfast and dinner · pine hotel +
Morning

Leave the house at 8:30. The climb to Gulmarg is about two hours. Rooms are usually ready by noon. If not, bags stay with the desk and you walk.

Afternoon

The meadow on foot, under 4 km. The gondola is optional and not in the base price. It closes in high wind, which happens. Phase 2 is the add-on on this page.

4 Across the valleyPahalgam · about 4 hours in the car · breakfast and dinner · Lidder hotel +
Morning

A short walk if the weather is kind, then the vehicle. This is the long day: Gulmarg to Pahalgam is about four hours, with a stop in Srinagar for lunch you buy yourself.

Evening

Check in on the Lidder. Dinner is at the hotel. There is no second sightseeing stop after a day in the car.

This is the day people wish a brochure had mentioned. It is a transfer, not a scenic stroll. The vehicle stops once. Children do better with a book and a snack from Srinagar.

5 The Lidder, slowlyPahalgam · short drives · breakfast and dinner · same hotel +
Morning

Betaab Valley by car, then an easy walk along the river, about 3 km. You can stay at the hotel if you would rather not go. Tell the guide at breakfast.

Afternoon

Free. Aru is a short extra drive if the group wants it and the road is open. It is not a promised stop. Pony rides are offered on the spot and are not in this price.

6 Back to SrinagarAbout 2.5 hours · breakfast · airport or Boulevard Road +
Morning

Leave Pahalgam after breakfast. An 11:30 airport drop suits a flight after 13:30. Earlier flights need a note when you request, and we leave earlier. A drop at Dal Lake House is the other choice.

End

The tour ends at the airport door or the house lobby. Flights are not included.

Where you sleep

These are the hotels on this departure. If one is full, we write before we move you, with the name of the replacement. We do not swap you quietly.

Dal Lake House

Nights 1 and 2 · Boulevard Road, Srinagar · lake-facing room · twin or single

The same house as our Srinagar stay. Kahwa on the deck, a jetty for the shikara, and a desk that actually holds a laptop. The boulevard is noisy until about ten.

Pine Hotel, Gulmarg

Night 3 · meadow edge · heater in winter · twin or single

A small hotel, not a resort with a lobby band. Rooms are simple, warm, and a short walk from the meadow. Dinner is here because the hill is quiet after dark.

Lidder Bank

Nights 4 and 5 · Pahalgam · river side · twin or single

Two nights so day 5 can be slow. Rooms face the water or the garden. Say which you prefer in the request. The garden side is quieter.

Before you go

FitnessEasy. You should be comfortable walking 5 km with pauses, and sitting for four hours on day 4. This is not a trek.
AltitudeGulmarg is 2,650 m for one night. Most people feel nothing but the cold. If you get headaches at height, say so in the request.
Who can comeAdults, and children aged 5 to 11 at 65% of the adult rate. Under 5 is not a fit for these roads. We will not take a booking that pretends otherwise.
Group sizeGroup dates hold at most 12, and only as many as the seats left. Private dates are 2 to 6, one vehicle.
RoomsThe rate is twin share. One traveler without a pair pays the single-room supplement. Children use an extra bed in an adult’s room.
CancellationFlexible is free until 7 days before the start. Saver is 8% less and not refundable. The card prints the exact day.
WeatherNovember is cold and usually clear. December brings snow in Gulmarg and a higher rate, because the rooms cost more to heat and hold.
PaymentThis demo takes nothing. A real request would be confirmed by email before any transfer.

Questions that change a booking

Does every date use the same hotels?

Yes. Dal Lake House, the pine hotel in Gulmarg, and Lidder Bank in Pahalgam. If a date is full at one of them, you hear the replacement’s name before you pay. Winter rooms are the same hotels with heating, not a secret upgrade.

What if the gondola is closed?

It closes in high wind. The walk on the meadow still happens. If you added the gondola and do not ride, that line should come off before you pay the balance. On this demo, untick it and the total updates now.

Can I arrive a day early?

Yes, at Dal Lake House, as a separate stay. This tour price starts on day 1. Add the airport transfer only if you want the car on the tour dates.

Is lunch really not included?

Really. Day 4’s stop in Srinagar is where most people eat. Other days you are near the hotel or the gardens. Carrying a snack is the difference between a calm transfer and a hungry one.

What happens on a private date if we are only two?

You still get the guide and the vehicle. The per-person rate is higher because nobody else is splitting them. You do not get a discount for empty seats. That is already the private price.
